HOW TO PLAY

SportsGuessr

01

Choose a sport

Each mode uses only players from that sport. Basketball will never mix in an NFL or MLB player.

02

Type a player

Enter the full first and last name. The board creates a tile for every character and uses a dark divider for the space.

03

Use the colors

Green is exact, gold means the letter is somewhere else, and gray means it is not used.

04

Unlock the team

After five incorrect guesses, the mystery player's current team is revealed.
